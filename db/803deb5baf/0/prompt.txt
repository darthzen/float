**New: `scripts/outpaint_grounded_caps.py`** — drives the pod through `sky360.outpaint_wf/_upload/_run` (same checkpoint and sampler as the deep-sky skies). Two things it does differently, both because of a failure I saw and fixed:

- **Caps are painted in an azimuthal-equidistant fisheye, not in equirect.** An equirect pole row is 360° of azimuth in one line, so the model paints horizontal streaks that map to a swirl exactly where you look when you look down.
- **Zenith and nadir are prompted separately** (your warning was right — the sky prompt does put stars in the playa), and the nadir hole is filled with *real ground texture*: the scene stands on a plane, so a target at ground distance ρ reads from a mirror-tiled orthographic tile of the real ground, which gives correct perspective — cracks get bigger toward your feet.

**Also changed:** `upscale/neural_sr.py` gained a tiled SR path — Bontecou at 9914px would have been a 786 MP single pass (9.5 GB tensor on an 8 GB card). It now runs 3×2 tiles landing directly on target. Geometry validated against a plain resize before spending pod time (mean abs diff 0.98, no join anomalies).

## Three real failures found and fixed

1. **Round 1 caps were garbage** — nadir came back a smooth lavender dish with concentric rings, zenith a black dot in the middle of the Milky Way. Cause: a blurred anchor at denoise 0.72 lets the *prompt* win. Fix was a better anchor, not more denoise (now 0.45).
2. **Bontecou's wedge outpaint did nothing** at denoise 0.50 — the streaked cross-dissolve anchor came back untouched. Replaced with a mirror-blend (your `_mirror_blend_seam` idea applied to a gap) at 0.62.
3. **A 1-pixel bug in the Death Valley master.** Its last two rows are stitch artifacts (row means 115.3 and 85.0 against a 112.2 neighbourhood). That put a hard dark ring right around the sphere at −31.9° *and* dragged the whole invented nadir 11/255 dark, because the cap anchor clamps from exactly that row. Now trimmed in `build_grounded_equirect.py`; measured join step went 9.83 → 0.83 against a 0.26 baseline.

While fixing #3 I hit a trap worth knowing about: `process_backdrops`' SR cache was keyed on filename only, so regenerating a master in place silently served the previous render three stages downstream. The key now carries a digest of the source.
